# Taktflasche final review

## Executive decision

**Use the standalone German demo for moderated concept testing. Hold commercial launch and production.** Start with German desk workers who already use a reusable bottle and sometimes forget to drink during focused work. Compare those interviews with a smaller gym sample. The concept is a refillable bottle with an adjustable light reminder and a removable module, designed to work without an app.

The German competitor listings establish price anchors from basic refillables to app-connected bottles. Official home-office and industry fitness data show accessible recruitment channels. Demand, willingness to pay, and repeated use for this specific concept still require primary evidence. See [market research](01-research.md).

## Strengths and risks

- The web demo illustrates the proposed benefit directly. The page identifies the offer as a concept and omits performance, health, price, and availability promises. Browser QA verified the interaction, keyboard activation, reduced-motion behavior, and offline operation. A retest confirmed that the light ring appears in view when triggered at 320 × 568 and remains visible without scrolling on the other tested viewports. Physical phones were outside the browser checks.
- The positioning gives participants a real choice to evaluate. Include their current plain bottle and phone reminder in interviews and the usage trial so the team can isolate the value of a light at the point of use.
- The unit arithmetic is consistent. At €49 and €59 gross, illustrative contribution after €10 customer acquisition cost is €3.68 and €11.88 per order, respectively. To retain the proposed €8 per-order contribution, the modeled acquisition-cost ceilings are €5.68 and €13.88. The figures depend on estimated landed cost, shipping, returns, payment fees, and modeled VAT. They exclude tooling, certification, and overhead. An extra €5 of product and returns cost would leave €6.88 at €59 after the assumed acquisition cost, below the €8 gate.
- Prototype QA validates the browser demonstration. Physical reliability, cleaning, charging, battery life, and applicable food-contact and market-entry requirements still depend on the final design and the selling entity. Establish the evidence before commercial orders.

## Next steps

1. **Interview 15 desk workers and eight gym users.** Ask for recent forgetting episodes and their current workarounds. Proceed if at least eight desk participants report two or more episodes in the prior workweek and an unmet need or unsatisfactory workaround.
2. **Confirm usability on physical phones during moderated sessions and test €49 against €59.** Keep the message and recruitment mix consistent across price variants. Agree up front on a qualified expression of interest, distinct from clicking the demo. Use the proposed 150 eligible visits per variant and 8% qualified-interest threshold as directional decision points. Only collect data after the live test's privacy arrangements are in place.
3. **Run a two-week physical trial once prototypes are safe for trial use.** Recruit 20 adults, track carry days and whether participants want to keep the reminder, and investigate cleaning, leaks, and charging. Seek 12 of 20 users carrying the bottle at least six of ten intended weekdays and 12 of 20 preferring to keep the reminder.
4. **Decide on a limited commercial pilot with verified inputs.** Obtain supplier quotes and field evidence on acquisition and returns. Clear applicable product, packaging, electronics, and battery obligations and confirm the €8 expected contribution threshold before taking orders.
