# German market-entry strategy

**Decision** Test a standalone reminder bottle with desk-based workers in Germany before committing to production.

## Positioning and scope

**German positioning** „Deine Trinkpause im Blick.“ A reusable stainless-steel bottle with an adjustable light reminder in a removable module. It is designed to work without an app, account, Bluetooth, or drink tracking. Propose 650 mL and a rechargeable module as design targets. Verify cleaning instructions, runtime, durability, and reminder controls before making product claims. Treat capacity, materials, and configuration as hypotheses.

The use case is getting back to a refill routine after long stretches of focused work. An LED on the bottle gives the reminder where the user can act on it. Removable electronics are intended to simplify cleaning. Compare this concept with a plain bottle and phone reminders, alongside app-connected alternatives. Germany's tap-water context supports a refill message. Competitor listings show a wide price range and leave room to test whether customers will pay for a simpler reminder. See [market research](01-research.md).

| Priority | Segment | Need and recruitment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | German hybrid and office workers who bring a reusable bottle and regularly forget to drink at their desk | Recruit through coworking spaces and workday-routine communities. Ask about the most recent forgetting episode and what they tried. |
| Secondary | Regular gym users who refill a bottle and prefer minimal setup | Recruit through a small number of independent gyms. Measure preference separately since exercise routines may already provide cues to drink. |

Destatis reports that 25% of employed people in Germany worked from home at least occasionally in 2025. This provides context for the desk-worker recruitment channel. [Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2026/04/PD26_N024_13.html)

## Pricing and economics

Use **€49 and €59** as randomized indicative gross prices in a German-language concept test. Hold visuals, offer and traffic source consistent, state that availability and specifications are provisional, and use a clear expression-of-interest action without checkout. Measure qualified interest per eligible visitor and by segment. Compare strongest objections and interest at both prices before committing to a hardware scope.

## Acquisition and decision gates

The figures below are proposed internal thresholds, not forecasts or statistical proof.

| Step | Channel and test | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Problem discovery | 15 interviews with desk-based refillers and 8 with gym refillers. Include current phone-timer and plain-bottle users. | Continue if at least 8 of 15 desk participants recall two or more forgetting episodes in the prior workweek and can describe an unsatisfactory workaround or unmet need. |
| 2. Concept and price | German landing page. Recruit via coworking and wellness communities first. Add small, capped search or social tests after checking recruitment quality. Split visitors evenly and log price assignment for the real test. | Aim for 150 eligible unique visits per price, at least 8% qualified interest at one price, and clear understanding of the app-free proposition. Treat small differences as directional. Recruit pilot users from the stronger segment. |
| 3. Prototype use | Run a two-week supervised trial with 20 adults and compare with their usual bottle routine. Log carry days, reminder use, leaks, and cleaning feedback. | Require at least 12 of 20 to carry it on 6 of 10 intended weekdays and at least 12 to prefer retaining the reminder. Resolve critical leakage, charging, or cleaning issues before further testing. |
| 4. Commercial pilot | Obtain supplier quotes, complete applicable pre-sale safety and compliance work, then use a limited German DTC batch. Test partners and marketplaces after direct fulfilment and support stabilize. | Require at least €8 expected contribution per order after measured CAC and returns allowance at the selected price, plus completed applicable compliance checks and support readiness. |

## Risk decisions

- **Commodity substitute.** Check whether the reminder still adds value after novelty fades. If continued use is weak, revisit the cue or stop before tooling.
- **Hardware and compliance.** Validate materials and food contact, water ingress, repeated washing, charging safety and battery life. Assess applicable product safety, packaging, electronic and battery obligations against the final design and selling entity. See the official links in [market research](01-research.md).
- **Concept communication.** Use German plain language and label each prototype call to action as a demo. Avoid medical benefits, guaranteed behavior changes, public sales and data collection in the standalone file. Review privacy before adding any analytics or signup to a live test.
- **Price and acquisition.** Manage test spend by source and avoid treating click intent as a sale. If customer acquisition or failure rates erode contribution, prioritize lower-cost recruitment, product economics, or revised scope before widening distribution.
